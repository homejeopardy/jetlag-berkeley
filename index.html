Hide + SeekBerkeley

Maps and a phone companion for playing Jet Lag: The Game's Hide + Seek around the East Bay. Every bus stop and rail station in the zone, drawn to be read at a glance from a train platform.

During a round

The companion app relays questions between the hider and the seekers: seekers send from the real question deck, the hider answers, and timers, cards and the feed stay in sync across phones. It needs the claude.ai artifact runtime for its shared datastore, so it is not served from here — see the repository for how to point it at your own backend.
